## Approved source and icon generation

The attached approved PNG is **1254 × 1254**, square, RGB. Independent review
found that the initial RGBA preparation retained an unwanted opaque black canvas
outside the intended rounded tile. That canvas has now been removed.

The corrected tracked source, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/icon.png`, is a
**1068 × 1068 RGBA PNG**. The central tile's connected silhouette was isolated
using its contrast with the external canvas. Enclosed dark areas were filled
into the opaque body mask, preserving legitimate dark artwork. Local foreground
and canvas contrast supplied partial alpha only in a narrow outer perimeter band;
canvas texture was excluded. This is not a global dark-pixel transparency filter.

The visible tile bounds in the original image are `[92, 90, 1160, 1112)`
(left, top, right, bottom), a 1068 × 1022 region. It was cropped without resampling
and centered on a square transparent canvas with 23px above and below, no added
horizontal padding, and no stretching. The original rounded shape and full
artwork are retained; there is no excessive transparent frame.

Retained RGB pixels are identical to the corresponding original crop; all cyan
nodes, connectors, hub, and symbol pixels remain fully opaque. The enclosed body
interior is opaque, with partial alpha confined to the antialiased perimeter.
Existing bundled Pillow/NumPy tooling was used; no dependency was installed or
added. No internal recoloring, sharpening, blur, new gradient, shape redesign,
or AI regeneration occurred.

The composition, circular hub, switch/shuffle symbol, six outer nodes, dark slate
background, cyan-blue accent, and original proportions are preserved.
